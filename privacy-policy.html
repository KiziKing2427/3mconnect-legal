3MConnect Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

3MConnect ("3MConnect," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using the 3MConnect application and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

By using 3MConnect, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. About 3MConnect

3MConnect is a social-media management and content publishing application designed to help users create, manage, and publish content to supported third-party social-media and business platforms.

Depending on the features available to you and the third-party services you connect, 3MConnect may allow you to create content and authorize publication of that content through connected third-party platforms.

2. Eligibility

3MConnect is intended only for individuals who are 18 years of age or older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal information to 3MConnect, please contact us at: 3mconnect.support@gmail.com

3. Information We May Collect

3.1 Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Login credentials or authentication information
  • Profile information
  • Account identifiers

3.2 Content You Provide

When you use 3MConnect, you may provide content such as:

  • Text
  • Images
  • Photographs
  • Videos
  • Captions
  • Hashtags
  • Links
  • Other information that you choose to include in a post

3.3 Connected Third-Party Accounts

If you choose to connect a third-party platform to 3MConnect, we may receive certain information from that platform according to the permissions you authorize.

  • Account or page identifiers
  • Basic profile information
  • Information necessary to identify the connected account
  • Information necessary to publish content on your behalf
  • Authorization information or access tokens
  • Information returned by the third-party platform after an authorized action

3.4 Technical Information

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • General technical information
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Information about interactions with the application

4. How We Use Information

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide 3MConnect's features
  • Allow you to create and manage content
  • Publish content to third-party platforms when you authorize us to do so
  • Authenticate your connected accounts
  • Maintain connections with third-party platforms
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized activity
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve the application
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

5. Third-Party Platform Connections

3MConnect may provide integrations with third-party services.

When you connect a third-party account, you are also subject to that platform's terms and privacy policies.

Third-party platforms may independently collect, use, store, or process your information according to their own policies.

6. Social-Media Publishing

If you authorize 3MConnect to publish content to a connected platform, you instruct us to transmit the necessary information and content to that platform for the purpose of carrying out your request.

Once information has been transmitted to a third-party platform, that platform's own terms and privacy policy govern its handling of the information.

7. Access Tokens and Authorization Information

When you connect a third-party account, the applicable platform may provide 3MConnect with authorization credentials, such as access tokens.

These credentials are used only to provide the functionality for which you granted authorization.

8. Information Sharing

  • Third-party platforms that you explicitly connect to 3MConnect
  • Service providers that help us operate, maintain, secure, or support the application
  • Authorities or other parties when required by applicable law
  • Parties involved in protecting the rights, property, or security of 3MConnect and its users

We do not sell your personal information as a product to third parties.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain legitimate business purposes, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, comply with legal obligations, and protect the security of the service.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

11. Your Choices and Rights

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Withdraw certain permissions
  • Disconnect third-party accounts
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled

For privacy-related requests: 3mconnect.support@gmail.com

12. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your 3MConnect account and associated personal information by contacting: 3mconnect.support@gmail.com

Deleting your 3MConnect account does not necessarily delete information that has already been published or transferred to a third-party platform.

13. Children's Privacy

3MConnect is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age.

14. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence depending on where our service providers and third-party platforms operate.

15. Cookies and Similar Technologies

3MConnect may use cookies, local storage, or similar mechanisms where necessary to maintain sessions, remember settings, improve functionality, maintain security, or understand technical usage.

16. Third-Party Services

3MConnect may rely on third-party infrastructure, authentication services, analytics services, hosting providers, APIs, and social-media platforms.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, we may update the "Last Updated" date and provide additional notice where appropriate.
